Stash notes and check-ins

A small personal set of apps (StashNotes, StashFlow) for Windows, Linux and Android.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026.

  • Stash keeps your notes and check-ins on your own devices.
  • If you turn on sync, the apps store your data in the hidden app folder of your own Google Drive (scope drive.appdata). They cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Everything is encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM) with a key made from a passphrase only you know, before it is uploaded. Google, and anyone else, sees only encrypted data.
  • Stash has no server. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party, and nothing is used for advertising, analytics or AI training.
  • You can remove all synced data at any time by deleting the app data in Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps) and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
